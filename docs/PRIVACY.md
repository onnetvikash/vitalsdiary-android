# Privacy Policy — VitalsDiary

VitalsDiary is designed to keep health-related information under the user's
control at all times.

- **No internet access.** The app requests no `INTERNET` permission and makes
  no network calls of any kind.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.**
- **Local storage only.** Case titles, symptom entries, photos and voice
  notes are stored exclusively in the app's private on-device storage
  (`getFilesDir()` and a local SQLite database). Nothing is synced, backed up
  to the cloud, or shared with Devexis India or anyone else.
- **Camera and microphone are optional and on-device.** Photos are taken with
  the device camera app and voice notes are recorded directly to local
  storage; neither is uploaded anywhere.
- **Sharing is user-initiated only.** The only way data ever leaves the app
  is when the user taps "Export PDF" and explicitly chooses where to send or
  save that file via the Android share sheet.
- **Uninstalling the app deletes all its data**, consistent with standard
  Android app storage behavior.

## Not a medical device
VitalsDiary organizes information the user records themselves. It does not
diagnose, predict, or assess any medical condition, and it is not a
substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always
consult a licensed healthcare professional with questions about a medical
condition.
