# Store listing text

**App name:** VitalsDiary — Symptom Timeline for Doctor Visits

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Track symptoms with photos & voice notes. Export a PDF for your doctor.

**Full description:**
Ever sat down with a doctor and blanked on when a symptom actually started,
or how it's changed since? VitalsDiary fixes that.

- Log a health concern once, then add dated entries as it develops
- Rate severity, add a note, attach a selfie photo or a quick voice note
- See at a glance whether things are trending better, worse, or holding steady
- Export a clean, doctor-ready PDF timeline in one tap
- 100% offline — your data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it

VitalsDiary does not diagnose or predict any medical condition. It's a
record-keeping tool that helps you and your doctor have a clearer
conversation, built on real data instead of memory.

**Category:** Medical or Health & Fitness
**Content rating:** Everyone
**Privacy policy:** See docs/PRIVACY.md — no internet access, no data leaves the device except when the user explicitly shares an exported PDF.
